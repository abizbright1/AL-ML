  --- Methods ---
  Every configuration was trained 1 times with independent random
  seeds (42); the seed controls weight
  initialisation, the subject-level train/validation split and batch
  ordering. All randomness is seeded through a single entry point (set_all_seeds),
  including each DataLoader generator. Runs on Apple MPS are seeded but not
  bit-deterministic, as the Metal backend offers no determinism guarantee;
  seeding therefore makes runs comparable rather than identical.
  Metrics are averaged within a subject before any test, so the subject,
  not the slice, is the unit of analysis. Effects are paired differences
  (PathologyLoss minus L1) on the same subjects. Uncertainty is a
  percentile bootstrap over subjects (10000 resamples). Inference uses
  a run-level paired test on the per-seed mean differences, the seed being
  the unit of replication.
  Equivalence was assessed by two one-sided tests against an a-priori
  margin of 0.05 dice_et, so that the absence of an effect is
  reported as a positive finding rather than as a failure to reject.

  --- Results ---
  The PathologyLoss gain in dice_et differed between backbones (SwinIR gain - Uformer gain) by -0.028
  (mean over 20 subjects; across-seed SD n/a, k=1 seeds;
  90% CI [-0.051, -0.005]; Wilcoxon p = 0.084,
  rank-biserial r = -0.45; TOST against +/-0.05: p = 0.057,
  equivalence not supported).

  --- Limitation to state ---
  For SwinIR gain - Uformer gain, the data neither detect a difference nor
  exclude one of 0.05 dice_et. This is reported as inconclusive;
  it is not evidence that the two objectives perform alike.
